The Secret of the Dream

Our master artisans employ the traditional Mochitsuki method—a rhythmic process of pounding steamed rice with heavy wooden mallets. This ancient technique, preserved for generations, is what gives our mochi its signature elastic pull and cloud-like tenderness.

Each Strawberry Dream is handcrafted within hours of sunrise. We only use 'Amaou' strawberries, known as the king of berries, ensuring that every bite is a harmonious dance of tart fruit and sweet, silky white bean paste.
